# MarketStock: requirements gathering questions

Prepared by the analyst for the client interview (owner and assistant of a provision stall) and for each member's one hour field visit.

Rule from the course: ask open questions about things that already happened. "Walk me through the last time..." teaches more than "Would you like...?".

## Before the visit
- Ask permission to sit at the stall for one hour and to look at the exercise book.
- Take a notebook. Do not record names of customers who owe money.
- Write what you see as well as what you are told. People describe what they think they do.

## Questions for the owner

Products and stock
1. Walk me through the last time you ran out of a product. When did you notice, and what did you do?
2. How do you know today how much of each product is left?
3. Tell me about your last trip or order to your supplier. How did you decide what to buy and how much?
4. When did the price of a product last change? How did your assistant learn the new price?

Sales and money
5. Take me through one sale from this morning, from the customer arriving to the money going in the box.
6. How did you find out what you sold yesterday, and what you gained on it?
7. Tell me about the last time the money at closing did not match what you expected.

Credit
8. Who was the last customer to take goods on credit? What did you write down, and where?
9. How do you remember who owes you, and how much? Show me how you check.
10. Tell me about a debt that was never paid or that you argued about.

People and tools
11. Who else sells when you are away? What are they allowed to do and not allowed to do?
12. What phone do you use, and what do you already do on it during the day?
13. If you could change one thing about the exercise book, what would it be?

## Questions for the assistant
1. Show me what you write when you sell something while the owner is out.
2. Tell me about the last time stock was missing. What happened afterwards?
3. What slows you down most when several customers come at once?
4. What do you do when a customer asks to pay later?

## Things to observe (do not ask, watch)
- How long one sale takes, from request to change given.
- How many sales in the hour, and how many were written down.
- Whether the exercise book is filled in at once or later from memory.
- Interruptions: phone calls, several customers together, deliveries.
- How prices are found: memory, a list, labels.

## Documents to look at
- The exercise book: what columns it has, what is missing.
- The credit page: how a debt and a repayment are written.
- A supplier receipt or invoice.

## After the visit
Write half a page in `docs/field-notes/<your-name>.md`: the business, date and time, what you saw, three things that surprised you, and which requirement in `srs.md` each one supports or contradicts.
